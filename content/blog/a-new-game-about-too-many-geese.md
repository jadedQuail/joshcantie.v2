Happy November!

I am working on a new game and just wanted to put something out here since it's been a while. I plan on calling it Mystic Goose Dungeon and it's going to be Diablo meets Cult of the Lamb meets Pokémon Mystery Dungeon meets a million random intrusive thoughts that I have? I basically started with what I had in my little Catacombs game and am now adapting it to be bigger. I plan on working on this for a while, because I'm feeling pretty inspired and because I want to practice making some of the design decisions that can only come from getting "deep enough" into a game to have to deal with certain things. It's been a lot of fun so far.

Here's a little clip to show a bit of what I have so far. I am again using the incredibly cute and versatile art of Kenmi's [Cute Fantasy](https://kenmi-art.itch.io/cute-fantasy-rpg) and related packs.

The more geese the merrier, right? Right?
